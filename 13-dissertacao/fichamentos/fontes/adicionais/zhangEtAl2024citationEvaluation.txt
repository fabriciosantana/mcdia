   unsupported or unverifiable content, known
   as “hallucinations.” To mitigate this, retrieval-

   such developments, manually assessing how

   ment remains a major challenge. Previous stud-
   ies use faithfulness metrics to estimate cita-
   tion support automatically but are limited to
   binary classification, overlooking fine-grained
   citation support in practical scenarios. To inves-
